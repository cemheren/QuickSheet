Your Desktop as a Content Command Center

Upload schedules, subscriber milestones, video ideas, sponsorship deadlines, and streaming stats — always visible on your wallpaper. No browser tabs. No app switching. Just glance at your desktop.

dotnet run -- content-calendar.csv --desktop

The Content Creator's Problem

📅

Upload deadlines slip

Your content calendar is buried in Notion or Google Sheets. By the time you check it, you've already missed the optimal posting window.

🧠

Ideas get lost

You had a great video idea at 2am but it's trapped in a notes app you never check. Your idea backlog is scattered across 5 tools.

📊

Metrics need context

YouTube Studio is great for deep dives, but you want daily milestone awareness without opening another dashboard.

🤝

Sponsorship deadlines

Brand deals have hard deadlines — first draft due, revision due, publish by date. Missing one burns the relationship.

🔴

Stream schedule chaos

When do you go live next? What game/topic? Is the overlay ready? This info should be visible at a glance, not in Twitch settings.

🎯

Goal drift

You set a "1K subs by June" goal but forgot. Progress bars and milestones need to be in your face daily to stay motivating.

Useful Extensions for Creators

📰 quicksheet-news

RSS feed headlines in cells. Track your niche trending topics, competitor uploads, and industry news without opening a browser.

ext: news https://feeds.example.com/tech

✍️ quicksheet-words

Text analysis: word count, reading time, Flesch score. Perfect for script writing and blog post drafts right on your desktop.

ext: words "Your script text here"

⏱️ quicksheet-pomodoro

Focus timer on your wallpaper. Great for dedicated editing sessions — see time remaining without checking your phone.

ext: pomodoro 25

🖥️ quicksheet-sysmon

CPU/RAM monitor. Know when your video render is hogging resources or if OBS is about to drop frames.

ext: sysmon cpu ram

Get Started in 60 Seconds

1. Install

git clone https://github.com/cemheren/QuickSheet
cd QuickSheet
dotnet build ExcelConsole.csproj

2. Create your content CSV

# content.csv
Day,Platform,Video,Status
Mon,YouTube,Hook tutorial,Editing
Wed,TikTok,Quick tip #12,Filmed
Fri,YouTube,Auth series pt3,Scripting

3. Launch on desktop

dotnet run -- content.csv --desktop

# Your upload schedule is now
# permanently on your wallpaper!

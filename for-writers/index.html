✍️ For Writers & Bloggers

Your Desktop as a Writing Dashboard

Notes, outlines, word count goals, and deadlines — always visible on your wallpaper. No app to open. No context switch. Just look up and your writing world is there.

# Start writing on your wallpaper
dotnet run -- writing-dashboard.csv --desktop

📝 Built for the Writing Workflow

📓

Persistent Scratchpad

Jot ideas, snippets, and quick notes directly into cells. They persist across reboots — always there when inspiration strikes.

📊

Word Count Tracking

Σ column sums

Use column sums to track daily word counts. Set a NaNoWriMo goal of 1,667/day and watch your progress accumulate.

📅

Deadline & Calendar View

Lay out your content calendar as a grid — articles across columns, due dates in rows. See your entire publishing schedule at a glance.

🔗

Research Links

https://source-url.com

Paste URLs into cells — they become clickable hyperlinks. Keep research sources next to your outline.

📤

Markdown Export

--export-md outline.md

Export your grid to Markdown for pasting into blog platforms, CMS editors, or sharing with editors.

🔍

Full-Text Search

Ctrl+F

Search across all your notes instantly. Find that character name, plot point, or article idea you jotted down last month.

⏱️

Pomodoro Timer

ext: quicksheet-pomodoro

Built-in focus timer on your desktop. 25-minute writing sprints with break reminders — no separate app needed.

📰

RSS Feed Headlines

ext: quicksheet-news

Pull blog headlines and industry news into cells. Stay inspired without opening a browser or doomscrolling.

🔄

Undo/Redo

Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y

Accidentally delete a note? 200-step undo history means your brainstorms are never lost.

⚡ Why Writers Choose QuickSheet

👁️

Always Visible

Your notes live on the wallpaper — visible between windows, during breaks, whenever you glance at the desktop. No app to find or open.

🚫

Zero Distractions

No notifications, no social feeds, no plugin marketplace. Just your words on a clean grid. The ultimate distraction-free tool.

💾

Auto-Saves Every 5s

Never lose a thought. QuickSheet autosaves to CSV every 5 seconds. Your scratchpad survives crashes, reboots, and power cuts.

📂

Plain CSV Files

No proprietary format. Your data is CSV — open it in any spreadsheet, version it with Git, search it with grep. You own your notes forever.

🖥️

Cross-Platform

Works on Linux (X11) and Windows. Same CSV, same layout. Switch machines and your writing dashboard comes with you.

🎨

Theme It Your Way

Dark, light, Nord, Solarized, Matrix — pick a theme that matches your writing mood. Cycle with Ctrl+T.

🚀 Get Started in 3 Minutes

1

Clone & Build

git clone https://github.com/cemheren/QuickSheet
cd QuickSheet
dotnet build

2

Create Your Layout

Open a CSV and add your notes, goals, deadlines, and ideas in a grid layout.

3

Launch on Desktop

dotnet run -- your-notes.csv --desktop

4

Write & Glance

Your writing dashboard is now your wallpaper. Edit cells directly. Auto-saves in the background.

✨ Writing Workflows That Shine

📖 NaNoWriMo Tracker

Track daily word counts across the 30-day challenge. Column sums show cumulative progress. See at a glance if you're on track for 50,000 words.

📰 Content Calendar

Plan your blog posts, newsletters, and social content in a grid. Columns = weeks, rows = platforms. Move ideas through Draft → Review → Published.

🗂️ Chapter Outline

One row per chapter. Columns for: title, POV character, word count, status, notes. Reorder with Ctrl+K (duplicate) and delete.

💰 Freelance Tracker

Track pitches, assignments, rates, deadlines, and payment status. Use row products for invoice totals. Export to Markdown for invoicing.

🧠 Brainstorm Board

Random ideas, character names, plot twists, headline options — toss them into cells. Search later with Ctrl+F when you need them.

📚 Reading & Research Log

Log books read, articles saved, quotes collected. Clickable URLs link to sources. Build a personal reference library on your desktop.

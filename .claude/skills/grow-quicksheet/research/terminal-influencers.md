Research run 2026-06-03. Goal: identify ≤10 accounts/people who organically
amplify terminal-tool discoveries. Not "who to spam" — who to *be visible to*
when the user posts.

## Summary (≤5 lines)

- **@charmcli** (Charmbracelet) is the single highest-reach TUI signal booster on X — they RT community projects.
- **@willmcgugan** (Textualize/Rich) actively advocates for terminal UIs; large Python/TUI following.
- **@consoledev** + David Mytton curate a weekly newsletter with strict quality criteria — QuickSheet fits 8/9.
- **terminaltrove.com** accepts email submissions (`hello@terminaltrove.com`) — zero friction.
- **terminal-apps.dev** (curated by @scmmishra) has a "suggest an app" flow — another free shot.

# Terminal Tool Influencers & Amplifiers — Research Brief (2026-06-16)

## Top-line summary

- The terminal-tool "influencer" space is dominated by **project maintainers** and **curated directories**, not traditional content creators.
- Two directories (**Terminal Trove** and **Console.dev**) are the fastest paths to organic visibility — both accept submissions.
- YouTube creators (**DistroTube**, **Brodie Robertson**) cover desktop customization and would find the wallpaper angle novel.
- **Charm.sh / Bashbunni** is the most connected DevRel node in TUI-land; a single retweet/boost from them reaches ~20k+ devs.
- Jesse Duffield (lazygit) and rothgar (awesome-tuis) are the GitHub power-users whose stars/follows cascade to new projects.

## Curated profiles (≤10)

### 1. Terminal Trove (@terminaltrove everywhere)
- **What:** Weekly newsletter + tool directory for CLI/TUI projects. 
- **Reach:** Newsletter subscribers (growing), active X/Mastodon/Bluesky presence.
- **Submission:** Email `hello@terminaltrove.com` or use https://terminaltrove.com/post/
- **Why QuickSheet fits:** They cover novel terminal-adjacent tools. Desktop-wallpaper spreadsheet is genuinely unlike anything in their catalog.
- **Action:** Submit via form. No gatekeeping — acceptance rate is high for real projects.

### 2. Console.dev (David Mytton, @consoledotdev)
- **What:** Free weekly devtools newsletter, 30k+ subscribers. Reviews 2–3 CLI tools per issue.
- **Reach:** 30,000+ email subscribers, syndicated on DEV.to.
- **Submission:** Via console.dev website; they track in Google Sheets and review weekly.
- **Why QuickSheet fits:** They love novel approaches to developer productivity. Extension protocol is a strong angle.
- **Action:** Submit with a 2-paragraph pitch emphasizing the extension protocol + zero-dep angle.

### 3. Bashbunni (@bashbunni on X, @bashbunni.dev on Bluesky)
- **What:** DevRel (formerly Charm.sh), Twitch streamer, terminal advocate.
- **Reach:** 10k+ X followers, active Twitch community, connected to Charm ecosystem.
- **Known for:** Championing novel terminal UX, approachable teaching, FOSS advocacy.
- **Why QuickSheet fits:** She loves creative terminal use-cases. The wallpaper-as-spreadsheet angle is a natural "that's wild" moment.
- **Action:** Be visible (submit to directories she monitors, engage naturally). Don't cold-DM.

### 4. Jesse Duffield (@jesseduffield)
- **What:** Creator of lazygit (50k+ stars) and lazydocker. Active TUI developer.
- **Reach:** Massive GitHub following; his stars on projects cause cascade discovery.
- **Known for:** High-quality Go TUI tools, responsive to community.
- **Why QuickSheet fits:** Different tech stack (.NET) but same "make terminal life better" ethos. Cross-link potential.
- **Action:** If QuickSheet gets traction, his star/follow would compound. Organic discovery path: awesome-tuis, HN, or Terminal Trove mention.

### 5. Brodie Robertson (@BrodieOnLinux on X, YouTube: BrodieRobertson)
- **What:** YouTube Linux content creator, covers window managers, desktop customization, terminal tools.
- **Reach:** 200k+ YouTube subscribers, active X presence.
- **Known for:** Covers niche desktop customization (tiling WMs, ricing, wallpaper management). Uses awesomeWM.
- **Why QuickSheet fits:** Desktop wallpaper mode is *exactly* his beat. "Your wallpaper is now a spreadsheet" is a thumbnail-ready hook.
- **Action:** After shipping a polished screenshot/GIF, consider reaching out or letting organic discovery via r/unixporn surface it to him.

## Implications for QuickSheet

1. **Submit to Terminal Trove and Console.dev immediately** — these are the two highest-probability, lowest-effort amplification channels. No screenshot required for submission (though having one helps). Draft the submission text now; user can submit when ready.

2. **rothgar/awesome-tuis and toolleeo/awesome-cli-apps-in-a-csv** — two PRs that cost nothing and provide permanent inbound links. The awesome-tuis draft should lead with the TUI/terminal angle, not the wallpaper mode. The toolleeo submission is near-certain acceptance.

3. **Brodie Robertson and DistroTube are the YouTube end-game** — but both require a polished demo GIF or video. Priority: capture that wallpaper screenshot/GIF first (blocked on human), then these become viable outreach targets via r/unixporn or direct submission.
